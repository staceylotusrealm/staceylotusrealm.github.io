HOW TO FILL THEM IN
-------------------

Open index.html in any text editor.

Everything marked in yellow on the page is a placeholder.
Search the file for  class="todo"  to find each one.
Delete the  <span class="todo"> ... </span>  wrapper once you
replace the words inside it, and the yellow marking goes away.

Near the bottom of the file there is a block that looks like this:

    const CONFIG = {
      email: "",
      formEndpoint: "",
      instagram: "https://www.instagram.com/staceylotusrealm",
      facebook:  "https://www.facebook.com/staceylotusrealm"
    };

Put Stacey's email between the first pair of quote marks.
That switches on the email link and the contact form.


THE CONTACT FORM
----------------

As it stands, the form opens the visitor's own email app with
the message already written out, ready to send. That works
everywhere and costs nothing.

If Stacey would rather have messages land in her inbox on their
own, sign up free at formspree.io, and paste the address it gives
you into  formEndpoint  in the block above. The form then sends
by itself and shows a thank-you.


PUTTING IT ONLINE
-----------------

Drag this whole folder onto netlify.com/drop and it is live in
about ten seconds, on a free address. A proper domain name
(staceylotusrealm.com or similar) can be pointed at it later.
